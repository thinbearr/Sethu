I am [your name], an independent developer from [city, state, institution if any]. I am building **Sethu**, a free, non-commercial assistant that helps citizens, especially people who are not fluent in English, find government schemes they may be eligible for. A user asks a question in their own language (English, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Bengali or Marathi), and Sethu answers only from official scheme text, shows which scheme and section the answer came from, and tells the user to confirm details with the official portal or office.

I plan to submit this project to [the government body / programme you are submitting to].

Section "Limitation on Use" of the myScheme Terms of Use prohibits access to platform pages by automated tools unless expressly authorized by myScheme in writing. I therefore **have not built, and will not run, any crawler against myScheme**, and I am writing to ask for authorization through whichever route you prefer:

1. Access to the myScheme data through **API Setu**, or
2. Written permission to read scheme pages programmatically, at a rate and schedule you specify, or
3. A data export or other channel you recommend.

**What I would use the data for**
- Answering questions about scheme eligibility, benefits, application steps and documents, in eight Indian languages.
- Every answer would carry the scheme name, section, a link to the official myScheme page, and the date the content was last retrieved.
- The text would not be altered in meaning, would be attributed to myScheme and the owning Ministry/Department, and would come with a notice that the user should verify details with the concerned department.

**Commitments**
- Non-commercial, free for users, no advertising, no sale of data.
- Content refreshed on a schedule you approve, with schemes that are removed or changed on myScheme removed or updated in Sethu.
- No personal data collected beyond what is needed to answer a question; no accounts.
- Willing to follow any technical, rate-limit, attribution or data-residency conditions you set.
